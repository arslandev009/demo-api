How this demo works

Chat is what a patient sees. The assistant can check times, book, look up and ask for a cancellation.

Behind the scenes lists every tool the assistant used and every safety rule that stopped it.

Front desk is the clinic staff view. A cancellation is only a request until a person approves it here. You act as the staff for your own chat only.

Your chat has its own private calendar. Nobody else can see or change it, and it is forgotten after 30 idle minutes.

Try asking for a time the clinic is closed, asking for medical advice, or cancelling an appointment you booked a minute ago.
